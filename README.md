# UniConnect Community

A place for UniConnect students to ask questions, share feedback, report problems, and follow product updates.

## Choose where to post

- [Ask a question](https://github.com/YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME/uniconnect-community/discussions)
- [Share an idea or feedback](https://github.com/YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME/uniconnect-community/discussions)
- [Report a bug](https://github.com/YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME/uniconnect-community/issues/new/choose)
- [Read announcements](https://github.com/YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME/uniconnect-community/discussions/categories/announcements)

Choose the matching category or issue form after opening a link.

## Before you post

This is a **public community space**. Your GitHub username and anything you post may be visible to other visitors.

Please do not include:

- PRNs, phone numbers, email addresses, or other private student details
- Passwords, login codes, session cookies, or access tokens
- Private conversations or screenshots containing personal information

## How to get a useful answer

For a question, describe what you were trying to do and what happened.

For a bug report, include:

- The page or feature where it happened
- The steps that led to the problem
- What you expected to happen
- What happened instead
- Your device and browser, if relevant

Please remove personal information from screenshots before uploading them.

## Community guidelines

- Be respectful and helpful.
- Search existing discussions before starting a new one.
- Keep each post focused on one question or suggestion.
- Do not share someone else's personal information.
- Do not use this repository for urgent or private matters.
